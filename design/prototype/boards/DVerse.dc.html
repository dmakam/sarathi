Bhagavad Gita 3.42

इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः ।
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः ॥

Chant · 0:34
Say it

indriyāṇi parāṇy āhur indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ manaḥ
manasas tu parā buddhir yo buddheḥ paratas tu saḥ

Meaning

The senses are higher than the body, the mind is higher than the senses, the intellect is higher than the mind, and the Self is higher still.

Krishna names a ladder inside us. Each step is finer and steers the one below. Desire can rule the senses, mind and intellect, but it can’t reach the Self at the top.
